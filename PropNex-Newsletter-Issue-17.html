✦ What This Really Means✦ What This Really Means

「凝聚一群人, 改变一代人。」
这句话背后, 是什么? "United as One. Transform Generations."
What's really behind these words?

01
United as One
凝聚一群人
把所有相信这个市场可以变得更好的人聚在一起. 不同 patch, 不同背景, 但走同一个方向 — 提高市场规格. Bringing together everyone who believes this market can be better. Different patches, different backgrounds, one direction — raising the market standard.
02
Transform Generations
改变一代人
带动一代又一代的 negotiator 提高规格, 逐步改变市场长期形成的陋习. 我们留给下一代的, 是一个更专业、更值得信任的房产市场. Raising the bar for one generation of negotiators after another, gradually undoing the bad practices this market has carried too long. What we leave behind is a more professional, more trustworthy property market.
03
Consumers Benefit
世代受惠
当更多人愿意做到, 每一位进入房产市场的消费者都得到更好的服务. 这份健康的市场文化, 会传给下一代, 甚至更多世代. When enough of us hold the line, every consumer entering this market gets served better. A healthy market culture, passed down for generations to come.
